About the Game

Fish Fillets NG is a charming and challenging underwater puzzle game focused on physics-based interactions, cooperation, and logic.

Control two fish through dozens of handcrafted levels filled with obstacles, movable objects, and tricky situations where every move matters.

This macOS version modernizes the classic open-source game for current Apple hardware and recent versions of macOS.

Support

If you encounter a bug, have a question, or would like to send feedback, please contact:

📧 thierry.duchassin@gmail.com

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • Your macOS version
  • Your Mac model
  • A description of the issue
  • Screenshots if applicable

Privacy

Fish Fillets NG does not collect personal data, analytics, or tracking information.

The game works entirely offline.

🇫🇷 Français

À propos du jeu

Fish Fillets NG est un jeu de réflexion sous-marin à la fois amusant, exigeant et basé sur la physique.

Contrôlez deux poissons à travers des dizaines de niveaux remplis d’obstacles, d’objets à déplacer et de situations complexes où chaque mouvement compte.

Cette version macOS modernise le célèbre jeu open source pour les Mac récents et les versions actuelles de macOS.
