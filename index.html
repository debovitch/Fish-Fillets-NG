About the Game

Fish Fillets NG is a charming and challenging underwater puzzle game focused on physics-based interactions, cooperation, and logic.

Control two fish through dozens of handcrafted levels filled with obstacles, movable objects, and tricky situations where every move matters.

This macOS version modernizes the classic open-source game for current Apple hardware and recent versions of macOS.

Privacy

Fish Fillets NG does not collect personal data, analytics, or tracking information.

The game works entirely offline.
